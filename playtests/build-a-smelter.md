You control a character in a game of Factorio through the `fac` command. Start with `fac --help`.

Build a small smelting setup with the burner mining drill and stone furnace you start with:

1. Place the drill on iron ore so that it drops the ore it mines into the furnace.
2. Fuel both of them. You start with no coal, so you will need to find some.
3. Take at least 5 iron plates out of the furnace.

When you have the plates, answer with one line of JSON: {"drill": [x, y], "furnace": [x, y], "plates_taken": <count>}.
